# KPI Dictionary

## 📌 Purpose

This document defines the key metrics used throughout the **Advanced Sales Analytics V2** project.

The purpose of the KPI dictionary is to provide a consistent definition of each metric, explain how it is calculated, and clarify the business question it is intended to answer.

Unless otherwise stated, metrics are evaluated within the current Power BI filter context.

---

# 💰 Executive Performance KPIs

## Total Sales

**Definition:**
Total revenue generated across all order-line records.

**Calculation:**

```text
Total Sales = SUM(Sales)
```

**Business Question:**
How much revenue has the business generated?

---

## Total Profit

**Definition:**
Total profit generated across all order-line records.

**Calculation:**

```text
Total Profit = SUM(Profit)
```

**Business Question:**
How much profit has the business generated?

---

## Profit Margin

**Definition:**
Profit generated as a percentage of total sales.

**Calculation:**

```text
Profit Margin = Total Profit / Total Sales
```

**Business Question:**
How profitable are sales overall?

---

## YoY Sales Growth

**Definition:**
Percentage change in sales compared with the corresponding period in the previous year.

**Calculation:**

```text
YoY Sales Growth =
(Current Period Sales - Previous Year Sales)
    / Previous Year Sales
```

**Business Question:**
Is revenue growing or declining compared with the previous year?

**Interpretation:**
Positive values indicate growth, while negative values indicate a decline.

---

# 👥 Customer KPIs

## Total Customers

**Definition:**
Number of unique customers within the current filter context.

**Calculation:**

```text
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(Customer ID)
```

**Business Question:**
How large is the active customer base?

---

## YoY Customer Growth

**Definition:**
Percentage change in the number of customers compared with the corresponding previous-year period.

**Calculation:**

```text
YoY Customer Growth =
(Current Customers - Previous Year Customers)
    / Previous Year Customers
```

**Business Question:**
Is the customer base expanding or contracting?

---

## Average Revenue per Customer

**Definition:**
Average sales generated per customer.

**Calculation:**

```text
Average Revenue per Customer =
Total Sales / Total Customers
```

**Business Question:**
How much revenue does the average customer contribute?

---

## Average Order Value (AOV)

**Definition:**
Average sales generated per order.

**Calculation:**

```text
AOV = Total Sales / Total Orders
```

**Business Question:**
How much revenue does the average order generate?

---

# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 RFM KPIs

RFM analysis evaluates customers using three dimensions:

* **Recency** — how recently a customer purchased
* **Frequency** — how often a customer purchased
* **Monetary** — how much a customer spent

Each dimension is scored on a **1–5 scale**, with higher scores representing stronger customer value or activity.

---

## Recency Score

**Definition:**
Score representing how recently a customer made a purchase.

**Scale:**

```text
1 = Lowest recency
5 = Highest recency
```

**Business Question:**
How recently has the customer engaged with the business?

---

## Frequency Score

**Definition:**
Score representing how frequently a customer has purchased.

**Scale:**

```text
1 = Lowest frequency
5 = Highest frequency
```

**Business Question:**
How frequently does the customer purchase?

---

## Monetary Score

**Definition:**
Score representing the amount of revenue generated by a customer.

**Scale:**

```text
1 = Lowest monetary value
5 = Highest monetary value
```

**Business Question:**
How valuable is the customer in terms of historical revenue?

---

## RFM Segment

**Definition:**
Customer classification based on their combined RFM characteristics.

Segments used in the project include:

* Champions
* Loyal Customers
* Potential Loyalists
* Core / Regular Customers
* At Risk - High Value
* High Value / Irregular Customers
* Low Value Customers

**Business Question:**
What type of customer is this, and how should the business prioritize them?

**Note:**
RFM segmentation describes historical customer behavior and value. It is not a predictive churn model.

# 📈 Cohort & Retention KPIs

## Cohort

**Definition:**
A group of customers classified according to the month in which they made their first purchase.

**Business Question:**
When did a group of customers first enter the business?

---

## Cohort Month

**Definition:**
The number of months elapsed since a customer's first purchase.

```text
Month 0 = First purchase month
Month 1 = One month after first purchase
Month 2 = Two months after first purchase
...
```

**Business Question:**
How does customer activity change as the customer relationship develops?

---

## Retention Rate

**Definition:**
Percentage of customers from a cohort who remain active in a subsequent cohort month.

**Calculation:**

```text
Retention Rate =
Customers Active in Cohort Month
    / Customers in Cohort Month 0
```

**Business Question:**
How effectively does the business retain customers after their initial purchase?

**Interpretation:**
A declining retention rate indicates that fewer customers from the original cohort remain active over time.

---

# 📊 Growth & Time-Series KPIs

## MoM Sales Growth

**Definition:**
Percentage change in sales compared with the immediately preceding month.

**Calculation:**

```text
MoM Growth =
(Current Month Sales - Previous Month Sales)
    / Previous Month Sales
```

**Business Question:**
How is sales performance changing from month to month?

---

## Months with Growth

**Definition:**
Number of months within the selected period where month-over-month sales growth is positive.

**Calculation:**

```text
Count of months where MoM Growth > 0
```

**Business Question:**
How consistently is the business generating month-over-month growth?

---

## CAGR

**Definition:**
Compound Annual Growth Rate representing the annualized growth rate across the available historical periods.

**Calculation:**

```text
CAGR =
(Ending Value / Beginning Value) ^ (1 / Number of Periods) - 1
```

**Business Question:**
What has been the approximate annualized rate of growth across the available historical period?

**Data Completeness Note:**
The dataset does not contain complete fiscal-year boundaries. The final fiscal period is only partially represented because the source data ends in December 2017. Therefore, CAGR should be interpreted as growth across the available historical periods rather than a comparison of four complete fiscal years.

---

## Revenue Seasonality

**Definition:**
The recurring pattern of sales performance across months of the year.

**Business Question:**
Are there consistent periods of stronger or weaker revenue performance?

**Interpretation:**
Seasonality can help identify periods that may require additional inventory, marketing, staffing, or promotional planning.

---

## Growth Driver Index

**Definition:**
An indexed comparison used to evaluate how changes in customer count, order count, and average order value contribute to changes in revenue.

**Components:**

* Customer count
* Order count
* Average order value
* Revenue

**Business Question:**
What factors are contributing to changes in revenue?

**Interpretation:**
The index helps distinguish whether revenue changes are primarily associated with changes in customer volume, order volume, order value, or a combination of factors.

# ⚠️ Risk & Opportunity KPIs

## Revenue At Risk

**Definition:**
Revenue associated with customers classified as **At Risk - High Value**.

**Calculation:**

```text
Revenue At Risk =
Revenue generated by At Risk - High Value customers
```

**Business Question:**
How much historical revenue is associated with high-value customers whose purchasing behavior indicates potential risk?

**Interpretation:**
This is a segmentation-based indicator of revenue exposure. It should not be interpreted as predicted churn revenue.

---

## Revenue Opportunity

**Definition:**
Revenue associated with customers classified as **High Value / Irregular Customers**.

**Calculation:**

```text
Revenue Opportunity =
Revenue generated by High Value / Irregular Customers
```

**Business Question:**
How much revenue is associated with valuable customers whose inconsistent purchasing behavior may represent an engagement opportunity?

**Interpretation:**
This represents a potential business opportunity based on customer behavior. It is not a forecast of incremental revenue.

This is particularly important when interpreting fiscal-year comparisons and growth metrics.

# 📐 KPI Interpretation Principles

The following principles apply throughout the project:

### 1. Filter Context

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, KPIs respond to the filters and slicers applied in the Power BI report.

### 2. Historical vs Predictive Metrics

Most KPIs in this project describe **historical business performance and customer behavior**.

RFM segments, Revenue At Risk, and Revenue Opportunity are analytical indicators based on historical behavior and should not be interpreted as predictive models.

### 3. Growth Metrics

Growth metrics compare values across time and should be interpreted alongside the available date range.

### 4. Association vs Causation

Relationships identified through descriptive analysis, such as the relationship between discounting and profitability, should not automatically be interpreted as causal relationships.

### 5. Data Completeness

Partial periods can materially affect growth calculations. The dashboard therefore exposes the available date range to provide context when interpreting historical KPIs.
